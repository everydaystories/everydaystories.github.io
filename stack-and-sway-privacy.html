Stack & Sway — Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

Stack & Sway is a free arcade game published by Amos Nsimba (Everyday Stories Anim). This policy explains what information the app does — and does not — handle.

We collect no personal data

Stack & Sway does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. The app has no user accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind.

Offline by design

The game works fully offline. Any game progress — such as your daily streak and best scores — is stored only on your own device, in the app's local storage. It never leaves your device, and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Notifications

If the app offers a daily reminder and you allow notifications, those notifications are generated locally on your device. No information is sent to us or to any server to deliver them.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics, or data-collection services. Because the app collects no data, there is no data to sell or share with anyone.

Children

Because Stack & Sway collects no information from any user, it collects no information from children either.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever changes how information is handled, this page will be updated and the new effective date posted here before the change takes effect.
